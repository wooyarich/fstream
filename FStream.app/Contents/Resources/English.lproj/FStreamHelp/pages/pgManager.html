Streams Manager

This is where you manage your favorite web-radio stations.

  • The list shows the names and the URLs of your streams:
    • Double-click on a cell to change its contents.
    • To remove a stream, select the line and click the “-” button.
    • To add a stream or a folder to the list, click the “+” button.
      A contextual menu pop-up, and let you add an empty favorite, a favorite filled with the actual playing informations, a favorite from a preset, a favorite from your iTunes library or a folder.
    Once your favorites web-radio added in the list, you can access them via the upper half-circle in the main window, or via the status menu.
